About these numbers
Clear-sky model (real Singapore skies often cloudier). VSC = sky visible from window centre (CIE overcast); ADF = average daylight factor (BRE). Readings: reveals/ledges/furniture not modelled. The Room interior view draws the sun per pixel through the openings (window recess, frames and furniture cast shadows), so its patch can be smaller than the reading.

How to read this
15th of each month, 06:30–19:30, 15-min steps, at the selected window & floor. Amber = direct sun (brighter = more head-on); rose = sun in front but blocked by a building; slate = sun behind the facade. Tap a cell to set date & time. Plan: sun path (Jun 21 / Mar 20 / Dec 21 + selected date) around the viewer.

How to read this
Indoor average lux (room average, log scale), 06:30–19:30, selected room/floor/glass. Amber = selected date, sky blue = compare date (solid = clear sky, dashed = overcast sky: ADF × CIE overcast 300+21000·sin el). ≥300 lx = enough to work without lights; ≥3000 lx = glare/heat risk. Tap to set time.

Geo frame: URA Master Plan 2025 land use (data.gov.sg), OSM buildings, MP2025 HDB footprints. Origin = TRPF parcel centroid (1.29682N, 103.87826E). Trees: parks/open space (22m grid), roadside (16m both sides), TRPF lawn (procedural, not occluders). Clouds: cumulus field drifting ≈4°/h, count/greyness per Weather preset (visual only). Realism (visual only): facade/roof cast shadows, foot-of-wall contact shading, Fresnel sky reflection + mirrored buildings + sun glitter on water, glass-tower sky reflection + sun glint, traffic on roads/decks, ships under way, room exposure (outside washes out, window bloom). Room interior: whole unit traced from the HDB 4-Room Type 1 plan (walls, doors, baths, kitchen, shelter; floor finishes estimated), 2.6m ceiling, W1 0.55m parapet to 2.2m head, charcoal casement frames, clear glass. Roads: OSM motorway–tertiary; bridges/viaducts as decks (≈7.5m per OSM layer; Benjamin Sheares Bridge 16m approach / 28m main span), piers every 35m; tunnels (KPE, MCE) omitted. Heights: OSM height/levels where tagged; known CBD towers overridden; untagged estimated from parcel GPR (marked est.). New HDB from MP2025 footprints, storeys ≈ GPR×8. TRPF blocks traced from HDB floor plans, placed via the site plan fitted to the URA parcel (0.225 m/px). Marina East parcels are “subject to detailed planning” (no published GPR or height): base case GPR 4.0 on ~70% net site (estimate 3.5–4.5), 30–44F stepping down toward Founders' Memorial, 10-storey frontage; GPR 5.6 (Marina South GLS, Marina Gardens Lane) is an upside scenario. Built floor area never exceeds GPR × net site. New towers: 3.15 m/storey + 4.5 m lobby + 3 m roof. Heights are above ground; URA height controls are in m SHD (add ~4 m site level).
