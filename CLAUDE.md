## Geometry / physics
- Everything is a prism (plan polygon CCW, base..h). Spatial grid 40 m + DDA (`candidates`), `planIntervals`, `prismHit` (curvature-apparent heights).
- Earth curvature + refraction k=0.13 (R_eff = R/(1−k)); sea-horizon dip; Koschmieder haze T = e^(−3.912 d/V); NOAA solar position + Bennett refraction; Lambert shading; sun shadows.
- Storey (HDB: TRPF + viewer eye): 2.8 m f2f, void deck 3.5 m; roof(30F) = 87.5 m. New towers (N/S/EA/reserves) use one storey height `newRoof(f)` = 4.5 m lobby (`NEW_LOBBY`) + (f−1)·3.15 m (`NEW_F2F`) + 3 m roof (`NEW_ROOF`) → 24F 80 m, 30F 98.9 m, 50F 162 m (owner review 2026-10-25: new SG condos ≈3.1–3.2 m f2f). All heights above ground; URA height controls are m SHD → add site ground level (~+4 m, estimate) before comparing. TRPF: TRPF residential blocks carry an estimated roof crown `TRPF_CROWN` = 2 storeys (5.6 m) → prism top 93.1 m (estimate from renderings; affects sightlines). Rooftop water tanks (estimate from model photo): 3 octagonal cylinders per residential block, r 2.2 m, `TANK_H` 3 m (93.1–96.1 m), near plan centroid snapped to roof cells ≥5 m apart; kind trpf (in sightlines), `P.tank` skips window grid. Eye = slab + posture eye offset.
- Metrics: gaze ray hit, ground dead-zone, frame-blocked % (raster mask: new / TRPF / existing+roads), landmark 7-sample visibility, binding roofline + floors-to-clear (uses posture eye offset `state.eyeOffset`).
